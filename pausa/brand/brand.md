## What it is
**Disco Pausa** — a coaster-sized NFC disc that pairs with a free parent app.
1. Parent downloads the free app and taps the disc once to pair.
2. Parent chooses which apps get blocked (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, games) and when (homework, dinner, bedtime).
3. At those times the apps lock on the kid's phone. The only way to unlock is to physically tap the phone on the disc, which stays where the parent put it (kitchen, entryway, parent's nightstand).
4. Parent gets a weekly report: unlocks, hours recovered.

Monitoring framing: parents see *patterns* (when, how often), never messages or content. Consent-first: kids know the disc exists. That is the whole point.
