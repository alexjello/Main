Claims we keep to

Pausa blocks apps on a schedule and reports time. Never say it reads messages, tracks location or is unbreakable. “Horas recuperadas” means hours with the chosen apps blocked, as measured by the app. Works with NFC phones (most since 2016). Guarantee: 30 days, full refund, customer keeps the disc.
